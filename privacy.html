Card Shop — Privacy Policy

WD Card Shop LLC · Effective 2026-05-25

Card Shop is an iOS app published by WD Card Shop LLC ("we", "us") for card-shop operators to view their own sales, inventory, and watchlists on their phone. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it is stored.

What we collect

  • Account email and password. Used to sign you in. The password is never stored in plaintext; only a one-way hash, handled by Supabase Auth.
  • Your business data. Sales totals, order records, product prices, watchlist entries, and notes that you upload to your account from your own systems via the included Mac-side uploader. We do not pull data from your stores ourselves.
  • Operational logs. Timestamps of uploads ("pipeline runs") and standard Supabase request logs for debugging and abuse prevention.

What we do NOT collect

  • No tracking, advertising identifiers, or analytics SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or health data.
  • No data is shared with third parties for marketing or sold.

Where data is stored

All account and business data is stored in a Supabase project on AWS infrastructure in the United States. Each shop's rows are isolated from other shops' rows by Supabase Row-Level Security tied to the account that uploaded them.

Who has access

  • You, signed in to the app or the Supabase project.
  • Us, the WD Card Shop LLC operators, for support and abuse prevention.
  • No one else.

How to delete your data

From inside the iOS app: tap the profile icon in the top-right of the Revenue tab, then Delete account. This permanently removes your account and every row of business data tied to it. There is no undo.

Alternatively, email support@wdcardshop.com from the account-registered address and we will delete it within 7 days.

Children

Card Shop is a business operations tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

If we materially change this policy, we will update the date at the top and notify signed-in accounts at next launch. The effective date above reflects the current version.

Contact

Questions, data requests, or deletion requests: support@wdcardshop.com
