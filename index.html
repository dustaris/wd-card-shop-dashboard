What the numbers say
  • The giveaway is mostly reaching people already on your list. 172 of 215 entries came from Email. 36 entrants are new to your list and 179 were already subscribers. To find new people, share the giveaway outside your list.
  • Email has brought in the most new people. 14 new entrants came from Email. Keep using tagged giveaway links (ending in ?utm_source=instagram, newsletter and so on) so every post gets credit.
  • lgsdrops visitors are the most ready to buy. 33 visits led to 10 add-to-carts and 2 orders, the best order rate of any traced source. It is worth putting more restocks and deals in front of that audience.
  • Most carts are not turning into orders. 88 visits added to cart and 65 reached checkout, and 14 ordered. Shipping cost at checkout is a common reason people stop. Free-shipping messaging and the abandoned-checkout email are the levers.
  • 201 entrants have never bought from the site. 14 entrants have ordered before, worth $5,307 in total, and 1 have ordered since entering. An email to entrants during a sale, then a thank-you offer after the drawing, is the next step to convert them.
