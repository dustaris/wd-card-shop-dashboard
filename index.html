What the numbers say
  • The giveaway is mostly reaching people already on your list. 174 of 237 entries came from Email. 54 entrants are new to your list and 183 were already subscribers. To find new people, share the giveaway outside your list.
  • Email has brought in the most new people. 14 new entrants came from Email. Keep using tagged giveaway links (ending in ?utm_source=instagram, newsletter and so on) so every post gets credit.
  • lgsdrops visitors are the most ready to buy. 47 visits led to 16 add-to-carts and 2 orders, the best order rate of any traced source. It is worth putting more restocks and deals in front of that audience.
  • Most carts are not turning into orders. 170 visits added to cart and 113 reached checkout, and 27 ordered. Shipping cost at checkout is a common reason people stop. Free-shipping messaging and the abandoned-checkout email are the levers.
  • 223 entrants have never bought from the site. 14 entrants have ordered before, worth $5,955 in total, and 4 have ordered since entering. An email to entrants during a sale, then a thank-you offer after the drawing, is the next step to convert them.
