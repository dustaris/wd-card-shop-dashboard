What the numbers say
  • The giveaway is mostly reaching people already on your list. 137 of 162 entries came from Email. 21 entrants are new to your list and 141 were already subscribers. To find new people, share the giveaway outside your list.
  • Email has brought in the most new people. 9 new entrants came from Email. Keep using tagged giveaway links (ending in ?utm_source=instagram, newsletter and so on) so every post gets credit.
  • lgsdrops visitors are the most ready to buy. 23 visits led to 6 add-to-carts and 1 order, the best order rate of any traced source. It is worth putting more restocks and deals in front of that audience.
  • Most carts are not turning into orders. 56 visits added to cart and 45 reached checkout, and 8 ordered. Shipping cost at checkout is a common reason people stop. Free-shipping messaging and the abandoned-checkout email are the levers.
  • No orders are coming from desktop. Desktop had 506 visits and no orders. Some of that is likely your own visits and bots, but it is worth placing one test order on a computer to rule out a checkout problem.
  • 154 entrants have never bought from the site. 8 entrants have ordered before, worth $3,896 in total, and 0 have ordered since entering. An email to entrants during a sale, then a thank-you offer after the drawing, is the next step to convert them.
