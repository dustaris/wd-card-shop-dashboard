What the numbers say
  • The giveaway is mostly reaching people already on your list. 172 of 224 entries came from Email. 44 entrants are new to your list and 180 were already subscribers. To find new people, share the giveaway outside your list.
  • Email has brought in the most new people. 14 new entrants came from Email. Keep using tagged giveaway links (ending in ?utm_source=instagram, newsletter and so on) so every post gets credit.
  • lgsdrops visitors are the most ready to buy. 35 visits led to 11 add-to-carts and 2 orders, the best order rate of any traced source. It is worth putting more restocks and deals in front of that audience.
  • Most carts are not turning into orders. 101 visits added to cart and 71 reached checkout, and 16 ordered. Shipping cost at checkout is a common reason people stop. Free-shipping messaging and the abandoned-checkout email are the levers.
  • 210 entrants have never bought from the site. 14 entrants have ordered before, worth $5,307 in total, and 1 have ordered since entering. An email to entrants during a sale, then a thank-you offer after the drawing, is the next step to convert them.
