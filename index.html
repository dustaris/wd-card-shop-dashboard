What the numbers say
  • The giveaway is waking up your email list more than finding new people. 128 of 153 entries came from email, and 132 entrants were already subscribers. Only 21 are new to your list. To find new people, the giveaway needs to be seen outside your list.
  • Instagram is your best source of new people so far. 7 entries came from Instagram, 4 of them brand new, from only about 18 visits. Posting the tagged giveaway link in your bio, stories and Whatnot streams is the cheapest way to grow this.
  • lgsdrops visitors are the most ready to buy. 23 visits led to 6 add-to-carts and 1 order. That is a far higher rate than any other source. Find out where that link is posted and keep your restocks and Black Friday deals showing up there.
  • Most carts are not turning into orders. 55 visits added to cart and 44 reached checkout, but only 7 bought. Shipping cost at checkout is a common reason. Black Friday free-shipping messaging and the abandoned-checkout email are the levers.
  • All 7 orders came from phones. Desktop had 495 visits and no orders. Some of that is likely your own visits and bots, but it is worth placing one test order on a computer to rule out a checkout problem.
  • 145 entrants have never bought from the site. 8 entrants have ordered before, worth $3,896 lifetime. None have ordered since entering. A Black Friday email to entrants, then a thank-you offer after the drawing, is the next step to convert them.
